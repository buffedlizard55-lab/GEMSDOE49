Metric, hide-and-recover holdout, and uniqueness audit

The metric (exact re-implementation)

Distance-weighted Tversky index per the official spec: triangular kernel R = 300 m (3 px), α = 0.2, β = 0.8. With the α/β values the denominator collapses to 0.2·(T+S−M) + 0.8·|G| — prediction mass that does not cover truth costs 0.2 per unit. Our implementation (src/gems49/metric.py) is pinned by 14 tests: hand-computed cases, a brute-force oracle on random grids, masking semantics (catalogue pixels removed from all three sums; a masked prediction can neither earn TP by proximity nor pay FP), and the published identity. Masking semantics follow the organiser's staff (forum 11516 post 4): pixel-exact mask, identical to the training labels; perfect catalogue reproduction scores zero; near-trace predictions far from new truth are fully penalised.

Hide-and-recover holdout (preregistered protocol)

  1. The only labels we possess are the public catalogue — which live scoring masks. So each fold hides whole 8-connected catalogue components (≥25 % of the fold's catalogue pixels, selected by quadrant with seed 49) and scores emissions against the hidden pixels, masking the residual catalogue.
  2. Four spatial blocks: NW / NE / SW / SE quadrants (hidden pixels 7,336 / 3,159 / 2,427 / 2,452).
  3. A candidate may go near a submission only if it beats the single-layer gradient baseline (gravity, magnetics, topography — same smoothing scale, same dot budget) on the fold mean.

Stated limitation: hidden truth is catalogue geometry, so this instrument ranks placement rules, not genuinely new fault families (IR-32-PROXY-01). Numbers are instrument readings, never organiser scores.

Gravity support check (before trusting 100 m gradients)

Measured on band 13 (evidence/gravity_support_diagnostics.json): only 34.7 % of the raw 100 m gradient variance survives 300 m smoothing (28.8 % at 500 m, 21.3 % at 1 km), and the along-row semivariogram is still rising at 55 px (5.5 km) — the field is trend-dominated, consistent with a grid interpolated from stations spaced at kilometres. We therefore compute gradients of the field smoothed to the adopted σ = 300 m rather than of the raw 100 m grid. Manual verification item: the official station inventory sits in the INGENIOUS compilation (GDR 1391, DOI 10.15121/1881483); sandbox egress cannot fetch it, so the spacing claim is left as “station-scale ≥ several hundred metres, empirical” rather than a quoted number.

Uniqueness audit vs every prior submission (57 files)

Corpus: every submission TIF archived across the GEMSDOE1–47 family repos that corresponds to a score listed on the family sites, plus all recent research candidates (data/prior/manifest.json records file-level sha256 provenance). Three earliest submissions are not preserved in any family repo (flagged irregularity): the audit substitutes GEMSDOE data/evidence/baseline/submission.tif, GEMSDOE4 data/evidence/combined/submission.tif and 5GEMSDOE candidate_s5f_flagship.tif.

H49-B validation (top next hypothesis — preregistered gate applied)

Alteration gate (Th/K z-score × coupled score) was tested at three thresholds on the same folds, matched 40k budget (evidence/h49b_validation.json): coupled-only 0.0203 · z≥1.0 → 0.0179 · z≥1.5 → 0.0139 · z≥2.0 → 0.0102 · radiometric-only 0.0089. Every gated variant loses, so no submission slot will be spent on H49-B v1. The gate remains a live idea for a strike-extended v2 and for Final-Round expert review, where catalogue-shaped holdout truth underweights genuinely hidden altered systems.
