100 m pixels ≠ 100 m gravity observations

Resolution was checked before interpreting the gradients. The observed grid is smooth at short lags, but autocorrelation is not native station spacing.

What the official source says

USGS DS 234 describes the Nevada isostatic grid as derived from the complete Bouguer gravity grid. The companion Bouguer page states that product used 71,055 station measurements and was gridded at 1 km. The competition mirror does not identify its exact source/version or expose station coordinates, so this is a caution, not proof of a direct source match.
