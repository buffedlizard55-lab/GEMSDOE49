# Preregistered geological hypotheses (GEMSDOE49)

**Date frozen:** 2026-10-06 UTC, before implementing the candidate detector.

**Purpose:** choose one scientifically motivated, falsifiable candidate; compare it with single-layer gradient baselines on spatially blocked hide-and-recover segments before producing anything that could be called a submission.

**Evidence labels:** **OFFICIAL** = stated by an organizer/government data or publication source; **LOCAL-MIRROR** = a public owner-hosted copy whose bytes can be pinned but are not organizer-authenticated; **HYPOTHESIS** = a physical interpretation to test, not a fact; **BLOCKED** = listed source exists but its data payload/coverage has not been verified locally.

## Novelty audit scope

This checkout initially contained only a one-line `README.md`; it had no model, feature code, holdout protocol, or submission builder. The project history is therefore broader than this Git repository. Before ranking candidates, we reviewed the published material from the sibling `GEMSDOE32` and `GEMSDOE47` projects, including their hypothesis summaries and experiment reports. That review found earlier work on per-pixel multi-physics orientation agreement, cross-scale magnetic-edge persistence, long-range topographic scarp persistence, regional strain-rate gating, fault-tip continuation, and thermal/geochemical feature families. The top hypothesis below is **not claimed as new scientific theory**; the proposed implementation is an untested application of a known structural operator to this challenge's gravity, RTP-magnetic, and elevation fields. The audit is bounded to sources actually inspected; the downloadable-corpus audit later in the pipeline is the separate test of raster-content uniqueness.

## Top-candidate preregistration (H49-XG)

1. **Inputs.** Confirm band descriptions from the actual multiband GeoTIFF, its manifest, and the competition feature description. Do not infer band IDs from an old model. Record the raw raster checksum. The exact band numbers and any unavailability are to be recorded in the run receipt before scores are calculated.
2. **Resolution caveat.** The official [USGS Nevada isostatic-gravity page](https://pubs.usgs.gov/ds/2006/234/nv_iso.htm) identifies the source as a gravity-station compilation and describes the map as derived from a complete Bouguer grid. The companion official [Nevada Bouguer page](https://pubs.usgs.gov/ds/2006/234/nv_boug.htm) states that the statewide complete-Bouguer product is based on 71,055 station measurements and was gridded at 1 km. These sources establish that 100 m pixels must **not** be interpreted as 100 m independent gravity observations. The exact provenance and native support of the competition's `iso_grav_anom` band remain to be verified; accordingly the primary test uses a preregistered 1 km-class low-pass before differentiation and reports a 0.5/1/2 km sensitivity analysis. If metadata shows a coarser native source, increase the scale before looking at holdout scores.
3. **Operator.** Use central differences in projected metres. Apply normalized masked smoothing, robustly scale each gradient magnitude on valid support, and compute axial (sign-invariant) pairwise cosine alignment. Do not multiply anomaly values or assume density–susceptibility correlation. Orthogonality is written as a separate diagnostic raster/statistic and is never added to the evidence score. If any one gradient is effectively zero, that pair contributes no positive evidence.
4. **Comparators.** The locked competitors are single-layer horizontal-gradient magnitude fields from gravity, RTP magnetics, and smoothed detrended elevation, each processed at the same support and emitted with the same pixel budget and placement rule. The conservative baseline for each fold is the best of those three single-layer results; H49-XG must beat that strongest comparator, not a weak hand-picked channel.
5. **Holdout.** Form five spatially separated, whole-segment folds from the public fault labels; place a 300 m exclusion buffer between fold blocks and any training-visible labels. Candidate construction uses no held-out labels. For each fold, treat only its hidden segments as positive truth, retain the same valid footprint and false-positive accounting for both methods, and score with the official distance-weighted Tversky equation (`alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`, triangular support 300 m). Publish fold-level and pooled DTI, hidden-positive counts, and mass counts. Report that this is a *public known-fault proxy*, not the private new-fault target.
6. **Emission/budget.** Compare H49-XG and the single-layer baselines at a fixed, preregistered prediction mass of 37,654 cells (the user-reported count associated with the 0.2778 raster; its artifact/score mapping is not organizer-authenticated). Use identical known-fault exclusion, ranked score ordering, 300 m-aware greedy non-maximum suppression, and probability values for every arm. The final output budget may not be retuned on the locked holdout.
7. **Promotion rule.** H49-XG passes only if it beats the best single-layer baseline on pooled DTI, has positive paired delta in at least 4/5 folds, has no catastrophic fold collapse (delta below −0.01), and passes deterministic random/shift controls. Any failure, insufficient labels, band/provenance mismatch, or a uniqueness collision keeps the submission gate closed. A pass authorizes a research candidate, not a claim of public-board or private-set improvement.
8. **Uniqueness.** Before a candidate is presented as a submission, compare its SHA-256 and in-footprint prediction ranks with every prior raster that can be recovered from the user-listed GEMSDOE sites. Report exact hash equality, Spearman rank correlation on the common footprint, and top-0.5%, 1%, and 2% overlap/Jaccard for each artifact. A unique file hash alone does not establish a unique prediction.

## Decision rule

No score or artifact is called submission-eligible until the holdout, control, format, and uniqueness gates all pass. If H49-XG loses, the correct outcome is a documented negative result and a closed slot gate—not changing the threshold after seeing the locked fold or relabeling a prior mask.
