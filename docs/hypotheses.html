Hypothesis backlog and evidence status

The ranks below are qualitative priors for expected gain per implementation cost, not score predictions. Every geological explanation is a falsifiable hypothesis. No listed candidate is currently cleared for a DrivenData slot.

Evidence boundary. Official competition text says the public catalogue is incomplete and the test contains newly identified faults; staff confirms the catalogue mask is pixel-exact. The local label raster is not the private expert truth. A leaderboard/proxy score cannot prove that a geological hypothesis identifies a new fault.

1. H49-XG — scale-matched gravity × RTP × topography gradient alignment

  • Layers: owner-mirror band 13 `iso_grav_anom`, band 2 `rtp`, and band 12 `det_elev`; tags were read from the local 19-band mirror.
  • Physical hypothesis: a structure that juxtaposes density and susceptibility contrasts may express co-located gradient directions in gravity and magnetics, with topography as corroboration. Lithologic contacts, interpolation artifacts, and unrelated edges are competing explanations.
  • Operator actually implemented in the separate PR #1 code: pairwise axial `cos²` alignment, median across the three pairs, multiplied by the minimum robustly scaled gradient magnitude. It is not a direct score from the standard cross-product magnitude. The separate orthogonality diagnostic currently computes `sin²(theta)` without a magnitude weight; it is excluded from evidence and needs correction.
  • Scale caveat: the PR #1 artifact uses Gaussian σ=300 m. The official associated USGS regional grid README describes `GB_iso_grav_anom.tif` at 1 km, but the exact mapping to competition band 13 is unverified. Do not treat 300 m as supported native gravity resolution.
  • Measured status: PR #1's final artifact reproduces four component-sampled catalogue-proxy folds at DTI 0.02026648, higher than its three single-layer baselines on that protocol. The protocol is not a true spatially held-out block test and masks held-out truth cells during NMS. Separately, the task-branch five-block primary at σ=500 m and mass 37,654 failed its preregistered gate because one paired fold was −0.01180243 (limit −0.01). NO SUBMISSION CLEARANCE

2. H49-B — radiometric alteration halo × coupled edge

  • Layers proposed: GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th channels and a gravity/magnetic/topographic edge score. Public USGS metadata links the GeoDAWN survey; the local external raster payload is not present in this checkout.
  • Physical hypothesis: hydrothermal alteration may change K/Th response and be spatially associated with fluid pathways. Mineralogy, lithology, survey footprint, weathering and calibration are strong confounders; K mobility/Th immobility is not a universal diagnostic.
  • Prior receipt: PR #1's `h49b_validation.json` reports gated scores below its coupled-only baseline on its own component-sampled folds. The input payload and method have not been independently rerun in this audit; do not treat those figures as a validated spatial-block result.
  • Status: EXPLORATORY / NOT CLEARED. A future run needs locally verified inputs, a preregistered block test, and an alteration/lithology control.

3. H49-D — en-echelon scarp-pair stepovers

  • Layers proposed: 1 m lidar-derived scarp evidence plus independently constructed local orientation/structure measurements.
  • Physical hypothesis: paired subparallel scarps may bound transfer structures in stepovers; the connector may be unmapped. A morphology-only detector can also confuse drainage, lithologic boundaries, roads, or erosional scarps with faults.
  • Status: UNTESTED. The lidar product is not restored in this checkout and no pair detector or blocked holdout has been run. Prior project summaries are not a substitute for source/coverage verification.

4. H49-E — MT conductance corridors across basin floors

  • Layers proposed: band 17 surface conductivity, band 15 depth to basement, band 2 RTP as a possible veto.
  • Physical hypothesis: a conductive lineament beneath basin fill might trace a blind fluid-bearing structure. Conductive clays, basin geometry and data smoothing are major alternative explanations.
  • Status: UNTESTED. The band descriptions exist in the local feature mirror, but no detector, source-resolution audit or spatial holdout has been run.

5. H49-C — strain-rate ridge coherence

  • Layers proposed: band 4 second strain invariant and band 7 geodetic shear rate.
  • Physical hypothesis: coherent geodetic gradients offset from mapped traces may indicate deformation on unmapped strands. The supplied strain fields may be too smooth to localize individual faults.
  • Status: UNTESTED / HIGH RISK. The input tags can be checked locally; exact native support and localization have not been validated here.
