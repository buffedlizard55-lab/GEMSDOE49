Ranking rule

A hypothesis was only allowed to consume a submission slot if it cleared two gates, in order:

  1. Hide-and-recover. On a spatially blocked holdout — four folds, contiguous catalogue segments removed whole, 3,118 segments, 40,000 target pixels — the candidate must beat the best single-layer gradient baseline by a positive margin in the collared regime, where truth pixels within 2 px of retained structure are dropped.
  2. Not a relabel. Support Jaccard, top-k Jaccard at k = 5k/10k/25k and Spearman rank correlation against every prior submission raster obtainable must not exceed the rejection thresholds in scripts/uniqueness_check.py.

A holdout win is a screen, not proof of a leaderboard gain: no available local instrument ranks candidates in the same order as the live leaderboard (see the instrument warning). This is recorded as a limitation, not hidden.
